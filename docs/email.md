# Email

Detailed notes moved from CLAUDE.md. Read the section for the feature you are changing; keep it current when behaviour changes.

- **Email:** Brevo SMTP from `news@paxvelo.cc` (add DKIM/DMARC in Cloudflare DNS; SMTP login looks like `abc@smtp-brevo.com`). Bulk sends pause after 3 straight failures and resume with **Resume**. Failures: `journalctl -u paxvelo | grep failed`. The email banner is one picture (`email-header.png`: logo + PAXVELO on the dark band, made from logo.png and Poppins 500 at 2x) over a dark tile background (`email-band.png`), since Gmail's dark mode inverts colours but not images; `color-scheme: light only` keeps Apple Mail from darkening it. Every email has `Reply-To: PaxVeloClub@gmail.com` (`REPLY_TO`), since `news@` takes no mail; Cloudflare Email Routing could forward it too.
- **Emailing an issue** (`newsletteremail.go`): issue page → Email this issue: preview, Send a test, Send to members (published issues only). `issueEmail` writes the whole issue in email marks (emailfmt.go adds `*italics*`, `^^ kicker`, `---`) and sends it as a campaign tied to the issue (`Campaign.Issue`), leaving the Email members draft alone.
- **Automations** (`automations.go`, `web/templates/admin-email.html`): Email → Automations lists every email the site sends by itself as rows (left edge green = on, red = off, yellow = sent by hand; a switch turns each on or off in place, on the list and on its editor (`auto-switch` template; `autoTrigger.Who` says who gets each, subjects show filled in for Pat); the site-move row has Send to members, a member list inside the row) that open to details and a preview (fetched into `srcdoc`: the CSP blocks framing our own pages). Built in: RSVP reminder (see RSVPs), Confirm your email (switch off = `ConfirmOff`: new joiners start confirmed; `Store.ConfirmText`, pieces `{confirm_button}` `{pay_line}` `{rides_for_you}` filled by `fillConfirm`; renew/rejoin variants stay in code), Welcome (pausable), Site-move. Admins add their own (`Automation`: trigger from `autoTriggers` + days before/after); `automationLoop` runs every 15 min from 9 am club time, sends to `Emailable()` members once per occurrence (`Sent[memberID]` = the day it follows), only for days on/after it was made and within 7 days (`autoCatchUp`). Remove has Undo (`RemovedAuto`). **Renewal reminder**: an ordinary `Automation` (`expiring`, 30 days; `defaultRenewalBody`: `{dues_expire}`, Renew now button to `{pay_link}`, `{my_link}`) added once by `seedRenewal` (`RenewalSeeded`, so a deleted one stays gone); `expiring`/`expired` skip family riders (`FamilyOf`), so only the plan holder hears about dues. Every email box uses `mailCards()` (easy editor: cards for paragraph, heading, button, photo, list, line, club pieces; "Edit as text" remembered as `mail-editor`). Round-trip check: `f=document.querySelector('form.mail-form').cards; f.text(f.parse(src))===src`.
- **Sent log** (`emaillog.go`): every send (`Mailer.send`) is appended to `data/email-log.jsonl` with kind, recipient, subject and result (never the body); halves itself past 4 MB. Admin Email → Sent log (search, kind, failed only); Home warns about failures in the last 7 days.
