PAXVELO LOANER BICYCLE AGREEMENT, RELEASE AND ASSUMPTION OF RISK

IN CONSIDERATION of being loaned a bicycle owned by PaxVelo (the "Club") for a single Club group ride, I, for myself, my personal representatives, assigns, heirs, and next of kin, agree as follows:

1. ONE RIDE ONLY. The bicycle and any equipment loaned with it (the "Bicycle") are loaned free of charge for one Club group ride on the date I sign this agreement. I will return the Bicycle to the ride leader or another Club representative at the end of that ride. I will not keep it overnight, lend it to anyone else, or use it for any other purpose.

2. INSPECTION AND FIT. Before riding, I will inspect the Bicycle, including its brakes, tires, wheels, quick releases or axles, handlebars, seat, and pedals, and make sure it fits me and that I can control it. I ACCEPT THE BICYCLE "AS IS." The Club makes no warranty or promise about its condition, fit, or suitability. If anything seems wrong, before or during the ride, I will stop riding it and tell the ride leader.

3. SAFE OPERATION. I know how to ride and operate a bicycle like this one, including its brakes, gears, and pedals. I will wear a properly fitted bicycle helmet at all times while riding, obey all traffic laws, and ride within my ability.

4. MY OWN SAFETY. I AM SOLELY RESPONSIBLE FOR MY OWN SAFETY WHILE USING THE BICYCLE. I understand that riding a borrowed bicycle involves risks of serious bodily injury, permanent disability, and death, including risks from mechanical failure, wear, improper fit or adjustment, or a defect in the Bicycle or its equipment, whether known or unknown, and I VOLUNTARILY ASSUME ALL SUCH RISKS.

5. RELEASE. I HEREBY RELEASE, DISCHARGE, AND COVENANT NOT TO SUE the Club, its officers, directors, members, ride leaders, volunteers, and the owner of any loaned equipment FROM ALL LIABILITY, CLAIMS, DEMANDS, LOSSES, OR DAMAGES FOR INJURY, DEATH, OR LOSS ARISING FROM MY USE OF THE BICYCLE, INCLUDING ANY CAUSED OR ALLEGED TO BE CAUSED BY THE CONDITION, MAINTENANCE, ADJUSTMENT, OR FAILURE OF THE BICYCLE OR BY THE NEGLIGENCE OF THOSE RELEASED, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

6. This agreement is in addition to, and does not limit, the League of American Bicyclists release below. If any part of it is held to be invalid, the rest shall continue in full force and effect.
