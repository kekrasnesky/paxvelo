Club terms

Ride safely

  • Wear a helmet on every club ride.
  • Follow Maryland traffic laws: stop at stop signs and red lights, signal your turns, and ride with traffic.
  • Keep your bike in safe working order, and use front and rear lights at dusk, after dark and in poor visibility.
  • Ride predictably in a group: hold your line, point out and call out hazards, and ride no more than two abreast, single file when cars need to pass.
  • Keep at least one ear free of headphones.
  • Never ride under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

Know your ride

  • Choose rides that suit your fitness and skills, and ride within your limits.
  • Follow the ride leader’s directions, and tell the leader if you leave a ride early.
  • You’re responsible for your own safety, your bike and your way home, on group rides too.

Respect everyone

  • Be courteous to other riders, drivers, pedestrians and the people whose roads, trails and shops we share. This is especially important when riding on base.
  • Harassment, discrimination, threats and abusive language aren’t tolerated: on rides, at events or online.
  • Carry yourself in a way that reflects well on PaxVelo. We are stewards of the sport, especially in club kit.

Your membership

  • Sign the club’s insurance waiver every year before you ride. A parent or guardian signs for riders under 18.
  • Dues cover one year from the day you join.
  • Photos from club rides and events may appear on the club’s website, newsletter and social media. Tell a board member if you’d rather not be in them.
  • Your details are used only to run the club. Only the board sees them, and they’re never sold or shared.
  • The board may suspend or end a membership for behavior that puts others at risk or breaks these terms.
  • These terms may change. Members are emailed when they do.
