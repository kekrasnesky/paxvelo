As a member, you’re a steward of the sport. How you ride, and how you treat people on and off the bike, reflects on all of us. So we ask every member to:

  • Be kind to other riders, drivers, and everyone you meet on the road.
  • Follow the rules of the road.
  • Carry yourself in a way that reflects well on PaxVelo.
