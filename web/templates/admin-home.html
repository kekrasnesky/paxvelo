The big picture

A new member signs up

Every email the site sends

Invitations and RSVPs

For a meeting, party or anything else members should answer: make it on Email → RSVPs (name, day, place, last day to answer, and whether to ask what people are bringing), then press Write the invitation email. The email’s RSVP button opens each member’s own page, with their name filled in: they say whether they can come, who they’re bringing and, if asked, what they’re bringing, and can change it until RSVPs close. The place shows only from a member’s own link, so a home address stays off the public site. Members also see who’s coming, with guests and what they’re bringing (untick Members see who’s coming to keep that to admins), and get a Directions button (Apple Maps on iPhones, Google Maps on other phones, plus Waze) and Add to calendar, on the page and in the invitation email. Maps get the street address: a name before it (“Karl’s house, 12 Spoke Lane…”) is left out. The RSVPs page adds up who’s coming (with guests), lists who’s bringing what and which current members haven’t answered, and has a spreadsheet. Take answers off with a row’s Remove, or tick several and press Remove them in the bar that appears (like Members); Remove at the top deletes the whole invitation. Both have Undo. Pick its event under On the calendar and the club calendar shows the head count on it, who’s coming and what they’re bringing when you hover it (first name and last initial), and its page takes RSVPs with Directions, Add to calendar and Share; members who open it without their link can have it emailed. The day before, everyone who said I’ll be there or Maybe gets a reminder email with their answer, the place, a Directions button and their link to change it (Email → Automations → RSVP reminder, where you can change its words or switch it off; untick Remind them the day before to skip it for one invitation). Any email can carry an open invitation’s button: “RSVP: …” under Insert for each member, or a button’s Goes to list.

Where the rides come from

Photos

A member shares photos

  1. On Share your photos they pick photos from their phone (or drag them in), add captions if they like, type their name for the credit, and pick the album (ride or event) or name a new one. No account.
  2. The phone shrinks the photos and they upload with a progress bar. The site drops where each was taken.
  3. With the club PIN, they go straight into the album they picked. Without it, or with no album picked, they wait on Photos to review. Each person’s photos are one card, already ticked, with the album filled in when it can be: one press of Approve puts them in the gallery. Rejected photos are kept out of sight for 30 days in case of a mistake.

The gallery

  • /gallery is a grid of albums, each with a cover. An album is a grid of photos that loads more as you go.
  • A photo opens full screen with its caption and “Photo by …”. Swipe or use the arrow keys; Share sends a link to that one photo, and Download saves it.
  • Each photo is kept in three sizes, so phones load small files and big screens sharp ones.

Photos in the newsletter

  • The gallery is where the club’s photos live, and the newsletter picks from it.
  • In an issue, Choose from the gallery on a photo strip or a story opens the albums: tick photos and they go in, with “Photo by …” in a story’s caption.
  • Or start from the album: tick photos on its admin page and Add to newsletter. Right after approving, the card on Photos to review links straight there.
  • The newsletter keeps its own copy, so taking a photo out of the gallery later doesn’t change an issue already sent. Uploading a photo just for one issue still works; it doesn’t go in the gallery.

The club PIN

  • Set it on {{if .IsBoard}}Security → Club PIN & access{{else}}Security → Club PIN & access{{end}} (the gear in the corner) and share it with members, in the newsletter or a club email. When it’s empty it’s off.
  • It lets members post photos and add rides without waiting for an admin, and lets ride leaders step down or cancel. Ten tries an hour, so it can’t be guessed.
  • If it gets around, change it there and tell members the new one.

Who does what

Visitors

  • Read the home page, Our Rides, Bike Safety 101, the board, the newsletter and meeting minutes.
  • Find rides on /calendar, tagged Social, Intermediate or Elite, with route maps from Strava.
  • RSVP on Strava. The website doesn’t take RSVPs.
  • Browse the photo gallery by album, and share their own photos.

New members

  1. Pick Individual (${{.Guide.DuesIndividual}}) or Family (${{.Guide.DuesFamily}}) and fill in the join form, with one riding question, the club terms and this year’s insurance waiver.
    2. {{if .Guide.Stripe}}
  2. Every admin gets a “New member” email. The form goes straight on to the card payment page (Stripe), and back to a welcome page with rides that suit them.
  3. Once Stripe has the money, the site marks them paid by itself and they get the welcome email with the riding advice for their answer. Leave without paying, and the next day they get Finish paying.
{{else}}
  • Every admin gets a “New member” email. The member goes on to PayPal.
  • The first time an admin presses Mark paid, they get the welcome email with the riding advice for their answer.
    • {{end}}

    Members you add yourself or import never get the welcome email, and renewals don’t get it again.

    Members

    • Every club email has their private membership link: dues status, a PayPal button, this year’s waiver to sign, their details, and emails on or off.
    • The insurance waiver is signed once a year, and parents or guardians sign for each rider under 18 there too. Other adults on a Family membership each get an emailed link to sign their own, once the dues are marked paid. The {{if .IsBoard}}Waivers{{else}}Waivers{{end}} tab keeps every signed copy and shows who hasn’t signed.
    • First-time riders sign at /ride-waiver (or /loaner, with the club bike already picked): name, email, loaner or own bike, and a signature. A loaner bike adds the club’s loaner agreement to the League waiver. They’re under Waivers → First rides, and on the member’s page once someone with that email joins.
    • Every group ride’s page lists the loaner bikes marked Available on Rides → Loaner bikes. A rider picks one and signs the loaner waiver, until 8 pm the day before. The bike is held for that ride, it's in the next morning's summary to {{.Guide.RidesEmail}} (at once if the ride is today or tomorrow), admins with alerts on get a Confirm button on their phone, and Home shows it; Confirm or Decline it there and the rider is emailed. Cancel frees the bike.
    • Every member email has an unsubscribe link.
    • With the club PIN, their photos and the rides they add go up right away.

    Riders and ride leaders

    • Open a ride, type a name, press I’ll lead. The club inbox is told. No account, no Strava sign-in.
    • With their name and the club PIN, leaders mark their ride Ride is on or cancel it (with an optional note like “Rain”), step down, or add the Strava event link, under Leading this ride?. On or Cancelled shows in a banner on the ride page, on the home page’s next rides, on the calendar and in the calendar feed; a ride cancelled for today or tomorrow stays in the next rides, marked. Admins do the same on the ride’s admin page.
    • Signs to print (Rides tab) has QR codes for Join, the ride waiver and the calendar, one sign per sheet. On a ride’s page, Waiver code fills a phone screen with the waiver’s code, for a leader to hold up for a new rider at the start.
    • A ride’s Strava route (paste its link, or pick a club route) shows its distance and climbing at the top of the ride page. Rides in the next six days show the National Weather Service forecast for their start.
    • I’m in on a group ride’s page: a name, a pace group if there are several, an optional email for changes, and a First club ride tick. The page shows who’s going (first name and last initial) and the calendar shows the count; the same phone can say Can’t make it. First rides email the club inbox and show a Sign the ride waiver button. Cancelling or putting a ride back on emails everyone who left an email. Admins see names and emails on the ride’s admin page and can remove someone; emails are dropped 30 days after the ride.
    • Ride alerts (the bell on the calendar and ride pages) put a notification on a rider’s phone when a ride coming up is cancelled or put back on, with the leader’s note. On an iPhone the button shows once PaxVelo is on the home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen). Once alerts are on, tapping the bell lets the rider choose Ride changes (cancelled or back on) and Club news (alerts the board sends from Email → Send an alert, at most five a day, opening the home page, the calendar, a ride or any page on this site). Nothing links a phone to a member; Email → Automations shows how many phones have each on.
    • Anyone can add a ride. It waits for approval on the Calendar tab unless they typed the club PIN.

    Treasurer

    1. Card payments mark members paid by themselves. Treasurer → Payments lists each one with Stripe’s fee and a link to it in Stripe, and who started paying and didn’t finish. A refund is done in Stripe; then press Undo on the member.
    2. Paid another way (cash, check)? Open the member and press Mark paid.
    3. Renewals get Thanks for renewing, and a week after dues run out, Membership ran out (Email → Automations).
      4. {{else}}
    4. A “New member” email arrives, then PayPal’s receipt.
    5. Open the link in the first email and press Mark paid. The first time, that sends their welcome email.
      6. {{end}}
    6. On January 1 everyone shows as not paid. Filter Not paid and send a reminder with {pay_link}.
    7. Any admin asks for club money on Treasurer → Requests: I already bought it, or I want to buy something. Press Take a photo to snap the receipt with the phone’s camera (on a computer, the webcam), or upload a photo or PDF; then type the amount and what it was for, and pick the ride or event if there was one.
    8. Nobody picks a category: the site guesses one from the words (pizza goes under Food & Drink) and from where the treasurer filed similar requests before. The treasurer can change it on the request before approving. Board members see what’s left of that category’s budget as they type; going over only warns.
    9. The treasurer and the president get an email with the receipt and Approve and Reject buttons. The buttons open a page with the request and one button to press; nothing is decided by opening the email or the link, so email scanners can’t approve anything. The links work for 7 days and only once.
    10. Small amounts need only the treasurer’s OK: up to the Treasurer alone approves up to amount at the top of {{if .IsBoard}}Budget{{else}}Budget{{end}} ($50 to start; blank means always two), as long as it fits in what’s left of the category’s budget. When the treasurer sent it, the president’s OK is the one needed. Anything bigger needs both.
    11. Once approved it’s on Expenses by itself and the sender is emailed. Senders follow each request on My requests: Sent → Approved → Reimbursed (or Receipt added, for a purchase).
    12. Reimbursements are by PayPal only: no PayPal account, no reimbursement. They go to the email the admin logs in with, unless they type a different one under PayPal for reimbursements on Admins (the form’s Change link goes there). The treasurer’s To reimburse list says who to pay, like “Pay Allie $45.00 — PayPal allie@…”. Send it in PayPal and press Paid; no money goes through the website. Pressing Paid emails a reimbursement receipt to PaxVeloClub@gmail.com for the club’s records, and to nobody else.
    13. Reminders go by themselves: one to whoever hasn’t answered after 3 days, a Monday list of who still needs reimbursing to the treasurer, and one to anyone whose approved purchase has no receipt after 10 days.

    Admins

    • Needs attention lists rides waiting for approval, rides this week with no leader, stuck email sends, unpublished newsletter drafts, and new photos members shared.
    • Photos to review (under Website) is the queue for photos shared without the club PIN. Each person’s photos are one card, all ticked: untick the ones you don’t want and Approve the rest into the album, or Reject them. Every action has an Undo. Photos stay private until approved. Photos posted with the PIN show there too, marked as such.
    • Gallery: start, rename, reorder and delete albums; upload photos straight into one; caption them, drag them into order, pick the cover, or move them to another album.
    • {{if .IsBoard}}Security{{else}}Security{{end}} (the gear in the corner): admins and passwords, the club PIN, every other way into the site, and the full backup.
    • Email: club emails, and the welcome email with its four riding-advice boxes.
    • Newsletter, Rides, Pages, Home photos, Board, Instagram and Minutes go live as soon as you save.
    • The video at the top of the home page comes from Home photos → Top video: one finished video for computers (wide, best at 1920×1080) and one for phones (tall, best at 1080×1920), both exported as AV1 in an MP4 at about 3,000 kb/s. The site doesn’t compress them again: it only takes out the sound and the camera’s details (like its location), so they’re live as soon as they’re uploaded. Make the end match the start, since it plays on a loop. Phones and computers that can’t play AV1 (older iPhones and Macs) see the video’s first frame instead. With no phone video, phones play the computer one, cut to fit. The switch turns the video off, and the top photo shows instead. People who turn motion off on their phone always see the photo.
    • Three kinds of admin. Website admins run the calendar and rides, email members about rides and events, review photos and keep the gallery, read minutes, look members up and send their own spending requests. Board members (anyone on Website → Board) can do everything else too. Only the president changes who has which powers: the Board page, adding and removing admins, and the full backup.

    What runs by itself

    • Strava club events import every hour. Instagram posts refresh every 3 hours.
    • Email sends carry on in the background and pick up after a restart.
    • A change saved to GitHub publishes the site, and the old version comes back if the new one won’t start.
