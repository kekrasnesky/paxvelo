How it works

On Share your photos, photos go straight into the album the member picks (with no album picked they still wait on Photos to review). On Add a ride, the ride goes straight onto the calendar. Capital letters don’t matter, and each connection gets 10 tries an hour. If the PIN gets around, change it here and tell members the new one.

Other ways in

  • Admin accountsOn

    Each admin logs in with their email and own password. Add, reset or remove them on Admins.

  • Setup passwordOn

    ADMIN_PASSWORD in paxvelo.conf logs in as any admin: the emergency key for whoever runs the server. Change it there if it might be known.

  • Calendar API key{{if .APIOn}}On{{else}}Off{{end}}

    CALENDAR_API_KEY in paxvelo.conf lets another program change rides. Anyone can read the calendar without it.

  • Members’ own linksOn

    The private link in every club email opens only that member’s own details and dues.

How waivers work

The club’s insurance needs every member to sign the League of American Bicyclists waiver each year. A waiver covers January to December, so everyone shows as not signed again on January 1. New members sign on the join form. Everyone else signs on their own membership page, from the “My membership” link in every club email, and parents or guardians sign there for each rider under 18 in their household. In January, send an email from the Email tab asking members to sign this year’s waiver. Adults on a family membership sign their own, from a link emailed to them once the member’s dues are marked paid, and again each year when they’re marked paid and that adult hasn’t signed yet. “Not signed” lists members and those adults: the site can’t know about riders under 18 until someone signs for them. Signed waivers are kept for good, even if a member is deleted. Open one to print it or save it as a PDF.

How the site works

Who uses the website, what they do there, which emails go out, and where the rides come from. Share it with new board members: it’s the same for every admin.

Where the rides come from

Photos

A member shares photos

  1. On Share your photos they pick photos from their phone (or drag them in), add captions if they like, type their name for the credit, and pick the album (ride or event) or name a new one. No account.
  2. The phone shrinks the photos and they upload with a progress bar. The site drops where each was taken.
  3. With the club PIN, they go straight into the album they picked. Without it, or with no album picked, they wait on Photos to review. Each person’s photos are one card, already ticked, with the album filled in when it can be: one press of Approve puts them in the gallery. Rejected photos are kept out of sight for 30 days in case of a mistake.

The gallery

  • /gallery is a grid of albums, each with a cover. An album is a grid of photos that loads more as you go.
  • A photo opens full screen with its caption and “Photo by …”. Swipe or use the arrow keys; Share sends a link to that one photo, and Download saves it.
  • Each photo is kept in three sizes, so phones load small files and big screens sharp ones.

Photos in the newsletter

  • The gallery is where the club’s photos live, and the newsletter picks from it.
  • In an issue, Choose from the gallery on a photo strip or a story opens the albums: tick photos and they go in, with “Photo by …” in a story’s caption.
  • Or start from the album: tick photos on its admin page and Add to newsletter. Right after approving, the card on Photos to review links straight there.
  • The newsletter keeps its own copy, so taking a photo out of the gallery later doesn’t change an issue already sent. Uploading a photo just for one issue still works; it doesn’t go in the gallery.

The club PIN

  • Set it on Security → Club PIN & access (the gear in the corner) and share it with members, in the newsletter or a club email. When it’s empty it’s off.
  • It lets members post photos and add rides without waiting for an admin, and lets ride leaders step down or cancel. Ten tries an hour, so it can’t be guessed.
  • If it gets around, change it there and tell members the new one.

Who does what

Visitors

  • Read the home page, Our Rides, Bike Safety 101, the board, the newsletter and meeting minutes.
  • Find rides on /calendar, tagged Social, Intermediate or Elite, with route maps from Strava.
  • RSVP on Strava. The website doesn’t take RSVPs.
  • Browse the photo gallery by album, and share their own photos.

New members

  1. Pick Individual (${{.Guide.DuesIndividual}}) or Family (${{.Guide.DuesFamily}}) and fill in the join form, with one riding question, the club terms and this year’s insurance waiver.
  2. Every admin gets a “New member” email. The member goes on to PayPal.
  3. The first time an admin presses Mark paid, they get the welcome email with the riding advice for their answer.

Members you add yourself or import never get the welcome email, and renewals don’t get it again.

Members

  • Every club email has their private membership link: dues status, a PayPal button, this year’s waiver to sign, their details, and emails on or off.
  • The insurance waiver is signed once a year, and parents or guardians sign for each rider under 18 there too. Other adults on a Family membership each get an emailed link to sign their own, once the dues are marked paid. The Waivers tab keeps every signed copy and shows who hasn’t signed.
  • Every member email has an unsubscribe link.
  • With the club PIN, their photos and the rides they add go up right away.

Riders and ride leaders

  • Open a ride, type a name, press I’ll lead. The club inbox is told. No account, no Strava sign-in.
  • With their name and the club PIN, leaders step down, add the Strava event link, or cancel their ride under Leading this ride?.
  • Anyone can add a ride. It waits for approval on the Calendar tab unless they typed the club PIN.

Treasurer

  1. A “New member” email arrives, then PayPal’s receipt.
  2. Open the link in the first email and press Mark paid. The first time, that sends their welcome email.
  3. On January 1 everyone shows as not paid. Filter Not paid and send a reminder with {pay_link}.

Admins

  • Needs attention lists rides waiting for approval, rides this week with no leader, stuck email sends, unpublished newsletter drafts, and new photos members shared.
  • Photos to review (under Website) is the queue for photos shared without the club PIN. Each person’s photos are one card, all ticked: untick the ones you don’t want and Approve the rest into the album, or Reject them. Every action has an Undo. Photos stay private until approved. Photos posted with the PIN show there too, marked as such.
  • Gallery: start, rename, reorder and delete albums; upload photos straight into one; caption them, drag them into order, pick the cover, or move them to another album.
  • Security (the gear in the corner): admins and passwords, the club PIN, every other way into the site, and the full backup.
  • Email: club emails, and the welcome email with its four riding-advice boxes.
  • Newsletter, Rides, Pages, Home photos, Board, Instagram and Minutes go live as soon as you save.
  • Only the president can remove admins, delete members or download a full backup.

What runs by itself

  • Strava club events import every hour. Instagram posts refresh every 3 hours.
  • Email sends carry on in the background and pick up after a restart.
  • A change saved to GitHub publishes the site, and the old version comes back if the new one won’t start.
