# Our Rides

There’s a PaxVelo ride for every pace. Start times, routes, and any changes are posted on Strava, so check there before you roll out.

[See upcoming rides on Strava]({strava})

## Tuesday Night Ride

Fast-paced and built for race prep, or for anyone looking for high-intensity work. We usually ride on base, and sometimes head off base to mix things up.

- **A Group:** 24–28 mph
- **B Group:** 20–22 mph

## Thursday Night Ride

A fast, steady rotating paceline. It’s not a race, and we regroup at set points so nobody gets left behind.

- **Who:** PaxVelo members. [Become a member](/join)
- **Pace:** 20–24 mph average, building as the season goes on
- **Season:** March to early October
- **Start:** Park & Ride by NAS Pax Gate 2, southbound Rt. 235 near Great Mills
- **Route:** Out to Point Lookout and back, about 40 miles

## Weekend Rides

A variety of casually paced rides on weekends, and a great way to try your first ride with the club.

New to group riding? Read [Bike Safety 101](bike-safety.md) before your first ride.
