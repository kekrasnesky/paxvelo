## All Riders Welcome

PaxVelo is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit cycling club. We’re here for great roads, good company, and the community we ride through.

{home-photos}

### Ride With Us

![PaxVelo riders in club kit gathering for a group ride](photos/ride.webp)

PaxVelo riders come from all backgrounds and every experience level, and there’s a ride here for every one of them. Southern Maryland has endless roads to explore, lots of them with wide shoulders, and the loop at Pax River NAS is the safest place to ride in the area. Grab your bike and come ride with us!

[About our rides](rides.md) [Find a ride on Strava]({strava})

### Giving Back

![PaxVelo members supporting a local cause](photos/giving.webp)

As a nonprofit, PaxVelo exists to enrich the community we ride in. Each year we host the St. Mary’s Century, bringing riders from across the region to Southern Maryland, and proceeds support local causes chosen by our members.
